Microshot: precision aim trainer

Tiny targets right next to the crosshair. Precision over speed. Free in your browser, no download or sign-up.

Play Microshot

Needs a computer with a mouse. Your score can go on the global and weekly leaderboards.

What it trains

Micro-adjustments: tiny targets right next to the crosshair, for precise small corrections.

How scoring works

  • Hit: +10
  • Miss: -10
  • Streak bonus: every 10 hits in a row add +1 to each hit, up to +4 (×1.4)
  • Each run lasts 60 seconds

Tips

  • Use small, controlled wrist and finger movements; big arm movements overshoot here.
  • Slow down until you stop missing, then speed up again.
  • A lower sensitivity often helps for this mode.
