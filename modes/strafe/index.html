Strafe: tracking aim trainer

One target glides around the wall. Track it and click. Free in your browser, no download or sign-up.

Play Strafe

Needs a computer with a mouse. Your score can go on the global and weekly leaderboards.

What it trains

Tracking a moving target and clicking at the right moment, like an enemy strafing side to side.

How scoring works

  • Hit: +10
  • Miss: -10
  • Streak bonus: every 10 hits in a row add +1 to each hit, up to +4 (×1.4)
  • Each run lasts 60 seconds

Tips

  • Match the target's speed first, then click once your crosshair is moving with it.
  • Move smoothly from the arm instead of making small jerky corrections.
  • Watch for the turn at the end of each sweep: that is where the target slows down.
